% ── Navigation ──
\section{Navigation}

Five tabs along the bottom of the screen:

{\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Tab} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
  \midrule
  Me       & Profile, macros, body composition \\
  Rest     & Rest timer between sets \\
  Workout  & Weekly plan, exercise logging \\
  Water    & Water intake, creatine tracking \\
  More     & Weight, Food, PRs, Watchlist, Dues \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
}

% ── Me ──
\section{Me — Your Plan}

\screenshot{guide-me.png}{Profile and calorie target}

Your profile and nutrition plan. Shows \textbf{name, sex, age, height, weight, body fat}, \textbf{activity level}, \textbf{goal} (maintain / cut / bulk), \textbf{goal weight} and \textbf{formula} (Mifflin-St~Jeor or Katch-McArdle).

A calorie ring displays your daily target (e.g.\ 1680\,kcal) with protein, carbs and fat in grams and percentages. Tap \textbf{Edit} to change any value; the plan recalculates immediately.

\subsection{Details (scroll down)}

\screenshot{guide-me-macros.png}{Macros, energy and body composition}

Below the ring: \textbf{fiber} and \textbf{water} targets, \textbf{maintenance calories}, \textbf{daily deficit/surplus}, \textbf{expected weekly change}, \textbf{basal rate} across activity levels, and \textbf{lean/fat mass} breakdown.

% ── Rest ──
\section{Rest — The Timer}

\screenshot{guide-rest.png}{Rest timer — idle}

A countdown timer for rest between sets. Pick a preset (\textbf{1}, \textbf{2}, \textbf{3} or \textbf{5\,min}) or use \textbf{Custom} ($-$/$+$ in 15\,s steps).

\screenshot{guide-rest-running.png}{Timer running}

Tap \textbf{Start} to begin. While running: \textbf{Pause}, \textbf{Reset}, or \textbf{+30s}. At zero a chime plays with a haptic tap.

Also runs as a \textbf{Live Activity} on the lock screen and Dynamic Island. The background notification chime does not double up with the in-app sound.

% ── Workout ──
\section{Workout — Your Week}

\screenshot{guide-workout-week.png}{Weekly training plan}

Each session card shows a \textbf{green check} if done this week, the \textbf{session name}, \textbf{muscle icons}, exercise count and last-done date. Top-right: \textbf{Plates} (barbell calculator), \textbf{History}, \textbf{Week} summary. Tap \textbf{Edit my week} to rearrange, rename or add sessions.

\subsection{Inside a session}

\screenshot{guide-workout-session.png}{Session summary with body map}

Shows \textbf{total sets}, \textbf{weight moved}, \textbf{time}, muscle group breakdown with weekly-volume percentages, and a heat-coloured \textbf{body map}.

\subsection{Weighted exercises}

\screenshot{guide-set-logger.png}{Set logger — weighted}

Tap an exercise to log sets. Adjust \textbf{weight} (kg) and \textbf{reps} with $-$/$+$ or type directly. Shows your \textbf{last performance} with a suggestion and your \textbf{personal record} — beat it here and the PRs page updates.

\subsection{Bodyweight exercises}

\screenshot{guide-bodyweight-logger.png}{Set logger — bodyweight}

For bodyweight exercises (e.g.\ Pull-Up): an \textbf{Added weight} toggle enables logging extra weight (belt/vest). When off, no weight is saved — only reps.

\subsection{Exercise info}

\screenshot{guide-exercise-info.png}{Exercise info sheet}

Tap the info button (\,ⓘ\,) to see \textbf{how it is done}, \textbf{alternative names}, and \textbf{muscles trained} with targeting percentages.

\subsection{Exercise picker}

\screenshot{guide-picker.png}{Browse by body map or search}

Tap \textbf{Add an exercise} to browse 162 exercises by \textbf{body map} (tap a muscle), \textbf{Groups} (list view), \textbf{Search} (by name), or \textbf{Cardio} (heart icon).

\subsection{Importing a plan}

\screenshot{guide-import.png}{Import from JSON or AI}

Tap \textbf{Import a plan} from the week editor. Without a file: copy the built-in AI prompt, paste into any AI chat with your plan (PDF/Excel/Word/photo), and import the \texttt{.json} it produces.

% ── Water ──
\section{Water}

\screenshot{guide-water.png}{Water intake and weekly chart}

Tracks daily water intake. A \textbf{progress bubble} shows amount, percentage and what is left. Three quick-add buttons: \textbf{250\,ml}, \textbf{500\,ml}, \textbf{750\,ml}. A \textbf{weekly chart} shows daily intake as bars.

Top-right: \textbf{Adjust} (target, creatine) and \textbf{Log} (custom amount).

\subsection{Creatine \& Streaks}

Below the chart, a \textbf{creatine toggle} tracks daily creatine intake. Scroll further to see \textbf{streaks} — consecutive days hitting your water target and taking creatine.

% ── Food ──
\section{Food}

\screenshot{guide-food.png}{Daily nutrition tracking}

Tracks daily nutrition against your plan. Shows a \textbf{protein ring}, \textbf{calorie status}, \textbf{macro breakdown} (calories, carbs, fat, fiber as current\,/\,target), and a \textbf{Quick Add} section to search foods or add protein-only / calorie-only entries.

Top-right: \textbf{Adjust} (targets) and \textbf{Log} (full entry with all macros).

% ── Weight ──
\section{Weight}

\screenshot{guide-weight.png}{Trend chart and status}

Tracks body weight with a \textbf{7-day half-life moving average} (smooth line) and individual dots. Toggle \textbf{1M}/\textbf{3M}/\textbf{6M}/\textbf{All}. Goal weight shown as a green dashed line.

\textbf{Where you are}: trend weight, last reading, rate of change (vs.\ plan), and distance to goal.

\subsection{Logging \& profile sync}

\screenshot{guide-weight-history.png}{Update prompt when profile diverges}

Tap \textbf{Log} to add today's weight, \textbf{History} for past entries, \textbf{Adjust} for goal weight. If the trend diverges from your profile, an \textbf{Update} prompt keeps your calorie plan in sync.

\subsection{Reminders}

Enable in \textbf{Settings\,$\rightarrow$\,Weight}. Rings at 10:00 each morning, skips days already weighed in.

% ── PRs ──
\section{Personal Records}

\screenshot{guide-records.png}{PRs filtered by muscle group}

Lists personal records for every logged exercise. Filter by muscle group chips. Each row shows exercise name, equipment, time since set, and weight. Tap \textbf{Add} to enter a record manually.

\subsection{Sharing}

\screenshot{guide-share-card.png}{Share card with body map}

Tap a record $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Share this record} to generate a card with exercise, weight$\times$reps, improvement, \textbf{estimated 1RM}, progress chart and body map.

% ── Watchlist ──
\section{Watchlist}

\screenshot{guide-anime.png}{Watchlist with filters}

Tracks shows, films or series. Name defaults to ``Watchlist'' — rename in the tab bar editor (Anime, K-Dramas, etc.). Summary shows \textbf{titles}, \textbf{seasons}, \textbf{episodes}. Filter: All / Pending / Airing / Watched / Completed. Search by name or genre.

% ── Dues ──
\section{Dues}

\screenshot{guide-dues.png}{Monthly dues with status}

Tracks recurring bills and payments. Header shows \textbf{amount left this month}. Each due: progress ring, name, category, amount, due date with countdown. Overdue items shown in red. Reminders arrive 3 days before and on the day.

% ── More ──
\section{More}

\screenshot{guide-more.png}{Hub for secondary screens}

Hub for screens without their own tab: \textbf{Macro Calculator}, \textbf{Weight}, \textbf{Food}, \textbf{PRs}, \textbf{Watchlist}, \textbf{Dues}, and \textbf{Settings}.

% ── Settings ──
\section{Settings}

\screenshot{guide-settings.png}{App configuration}

\textbf{Navbar} — rearrange the five tabs. \textbf{Day starts at} — roll the day over at Midnight, 1\,am, 2\,am or 3\,am (affects totals, streaks and all daily grouping; confirmation required). \textbf{Apple Health} — toggle writes for Water, Food, Body, Workouts (write-only, never reads). \textbf{Screen settings} — per-screen options for Workout, Weight, Water and Plates.

% ── About ──
\section{About}

\screenshot{guide-about.png}{Privacy and credits}

Shows version, developer (\textbf{kowsyap}), GitHub link and issue reporting.

\begin{privacybox}
\textit{Everything you log stays on this iPhone and your Apple Watch. There is no account, no cloud and no tracking.}
\end{privacybox}

Apple Health: write-only. Food photos: on-device via Apple Intelligence (or Google if Gemini enabled). Watchlist posters: loaded from URLs you provide.

% ── Tips ──
\vspace{6pt}
\begin{tipbox}
{\normalsize\bfseries Tips}\\[3pt]
\textbf{Weigh in} first thing, before eating. \textbf{Log water} with quick-add taps. \textbf{Use the rest timer} every set. \textbf{Check the body map} after sessions. \textbf{Share PRs} — the cards look great. \textbf{Set day boundary} if you are a night owl.
\end{tipbox}
